About Our Engineering Framework

CondoSense was founded by senior technology infrastructure veterans to bring deterministic data intelligence to Florida's shifting regulatory landscape. In an era governed by stringent HB 1021 and Structural Integrity Reserve Study (SIRS) mandates, relying on manual document review or unverified digital portals introduces immense operational and fiduciary risk.

We don't build generic property management software. Instead, we develop ai agentic data-auditing tools engineered to inspect public registries, track corporate filings, and systematically index complex condominium declarations and bylaws. By converting vast, unstructured document pools into highly visible, actionable compliance data, CondoSense empowers portfolio managers, legal counsel, and board members to identify disclosure gaps and eliminate administrative strain before enforcement actions occur.
